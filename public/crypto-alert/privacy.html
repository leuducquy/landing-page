Crypto Alert Privacy Policy

This policy describes how information is handled when you use the Crypto Alert app.

Accounts and direct collection

Crypto Alert does not require an account or sign-in and does not directly collect personal information from you through the app.

Advertising and analytics

Crypto Alert uses third-party services to display ads and understand how the app is used. Depending on the service and its configuration, these providers may receive information such as device and app details, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, app usage events, and ad interactions.

Advertising providers may use this information to deliver and measure ads. Analytics providers may use it to report app usage and performance. The providers’ privacy policies describe their handling and retention of this information.

Your choices

Depending on your device and operating system, you may be able to manage ad personalization or limit tracking in your device’s privacy settings. You can contact us with questions or requests about your data.

Contact

Email leuducquy@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests. If you email us, we use your email address and message to respond.
